Vietnam’s industrial real estate market has grown significantly focusing on quality and sustainability. Green transition and ESG alignment attract global corporations like Samsung, Intel, and Unilever. Industrial developers incorporate energy efficiency, LEED Gold certification, water-saving, and solar-ready designs to meet manufacturer needs and long-term sustainability goals.
